## Parental leave

New parents are entitled to 180 days of parental leave, shared between
caregivers. The leave can start no earlier than 15 days before the expected
due date and can be combined with the annual vacation days.

## Remote work

Up to three days per week of remote work are allowed after the onboarding
period. Requests must be approved by the team lead and are reviewed every
quarter.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a headset within the first
week. Damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 5 business days.

## Discrimination

The company supports an inclusive environment. Any form of discrimination or
harassment is prohibited and should be reported to HR immediately.
